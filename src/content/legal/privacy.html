5. Software and AI-assisted processing

We use our own application to run recruitment. It holds role requirements, candidate records and assessments, and it uses AI-assisted tools to help our recruiters work consistently. In outline, those tools:

  • help structure what a role needs into a written, weighted set of requirements;
  • read the evidence we hold about a candidate — including CVs and, where an interview has taken place, interview notes or transcripts — and identify what supports or does not support each requirement;
  • produce fit indicators shown alongside the supporting evidence, how much of the requirement set that evidence covers, and how confident the assessment is;
  • record gaps where evidence is missing, and help produce interview questions aimed at those gaps;
  • re-run an assessment against the same recorded requirements when new evidence arrives.

These tools support our recruiters rather than replacing recruiter judgement. No decision about a candidate is made by software alone, assessments can be reviewed and produced again when further information becomes available, and a person is accountable for what we do with the result. Assessment processing runs only when a recruiter explicitly asks for a candidate to be assessed for a role.

We do not use personal information to train publicly available AI models. Our fair recruitment and responsible AI page explains the safeguards, and what we do not claim.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

We maintain an internal register of the providers we use, what they process and where. If you want the current detail for your information, ask us and we will tell you.

7. Where information is processed

We are a UK business and information is handled in the UK wherever we can. Some providers process information outside the UK, including in the United States and the European Economic Area.

Where information leaves the UK we rely on UK adequacy regulations where they cover the destination, or on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses, together with any additional protections the transfer requires.

8. How long we keep information

  • Candidate records: up to 2 years from our last meaningful contact, and longer where there is a continuing reason — an active application or placement, an ongoing relationship, an unresolved dispute, or a legal duty to keep records. See the candidate privacy notice.
  • Client and supplier records: for the duration of our relationship and for up to 6 years afterwards, for legal, tax and accounting purposes.
  • Enquiries that go nowhere: up to 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Website security and server logs: for the short period our hosting provider retains them for security and abuse prevention.
  • At the end of the applicable period we delete the record or remove the details that identify you.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to give you access to your information, correct it, delete it, restrict what we do with it, or provide it in a portable format. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests, object to direct marketing at any time, and withdraw consent where consent is what we relied on. Candidates can also ask a person to review an assessment.

Email privacy@surfacetalent.co.uk, or see privacy requests for what to include and how long we take. Requests are free. We normally respond within one calendar month; where the law allows an extension for a complex request or several requests together, we may take up to two further months and will tell you within the first month.

11. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss or damage. These include access controls so that information is reachable only by people who need it, encryption of data in transit, keeping credentials on the server side and never in your browser, and using established hosting and platform providers rather than running our own infrastructure.

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to people's rights and freedoms, we report it to the Information Commissioner's Office within the statutory deadline. If it is likely to result in a high risk, we also tell the people affected without undue delay, subject to the limited exceptions the law allows.

12. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, tell us at privacy@surfacetalent.co.uk. We acknowledge a data protection complaint within 30 days of receiving it, take appropriate steps to look into it without undue delay, keep you posted on progress, and tell you the outcome. See privacy requests for the detail.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. You do not have to come to us first.

If your concern is about our conduct as a recruitment business rather than about your information, you can report it to the Fair Work Agency, which enforces recruitment sector rules in Great Britain. See our modern slavery and ethical recruitment policy.
