This website stores nothing on your device. No cookies, no local or session storage. There is no analytics, no session recording, no advertising or social media tracking and no cross-site profiling. That is why you are not asked to accept or reject anything when you arrive — there is nothing to ask about.

1. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small file a website stores on your device. Related technologies — local storage and session storage — do the same job in a different way. UK rules on cookies also cover those, so this page describes everything this site stores on your device, not just cookies.

2. Cookies we set

None. We set no first-party cookies, and we load nothing that sets third-party cookies on our pages.

If you are used to a cookie banner on every site, its absence here is deliberate. A banner is required when a site wants to store non-essential information on your device. We do not store anything at all. If that ever changes, we will ask for your consent before storing anything non-essential, and this page will be updated first.

3. Other storage on your device

Also none — no local storage, no session storage, no browser database, no fingerprinting.

This is worth explaining, because it used to be otherwise. Until August 2026 this site kept two session-storage entries to remember which page and which marketing campaign an enquiry came from. They were first-party and deleted when you closed the tab, but UK storage-and-access rules cover session storage just as they cover cookies, and campaign attribution is not strictly necessary for showing you the page you asked for. Rather than put a consent banner in front of everyone for the sake of two entries, we removed them.

4. What is sent when you submit a form

This is about the information that travels with an enquiry, not about anything stored on your device.

If you submit a contact, talent-pool or application form, the submission includes, alongside what you typed:

  • the address of the page you submitted from, including any query string it carried;
  • any campaign parameters present in that address at that moment — utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content and gclid;
  • the referring page your browser reports, if any;
  • the time the form was started, used to filter automated spam.

This tells us where a genuine enquiry came from so we can reply in context and know which advertising is worth continuing. Because nothing is stored, it only reflects the page you actually submitted from — if you arrive on a campaign link and then browse elsewhere before enquiring, the campaign detail is simply absent. It is handled as part of your enquiry under our privacy policy.

5. Third parties your browser contacts

Some parts of the site are served from other companies' networks. Those companies necessarily see your IP address and basic request information, because that is how the web works. None of them set cookies on our pages, and none of them are analytics or advertising services.

Two things people often assume are here, and are not:

  • Our jobs list does not contact Airtable from your browser. Role data is held in Airtable, but it is fetched by our own server and passed to the page from our domain. Your browser never talks to Airtable, and Airtable sets nothing on your device.
  • Our forms do not use a third-party form service. They post to our own endpoint on this domain, which forwards the submission to our internal systems. No external form provider is loaded into the page.

If you click through to a booking link, that opens Google Calendar on Google's own site, where Google's terms and cookies apply. The same is true of any other external site we link to.

6. What we deliberately do not do

  • No Google Analytics, and no other analytics product.
  • No session recording, heatmaps or screen replay.
  • No advertising, remarketing or conversion pixels.
  • No social media tracking pixels or embedded trackers.
  • No fingerprinting, and no attempt to identify you across other websites.

We may add basic, privacy-respecting analytics in future. If we do, it will appear in this policy first, and anything non-essential will be off until you agree to it.
