# Keyword research — UK surface engineering recruitment

Researched 2026-09-02 for Surface Talent (`surfacetalent.co.uk`). Companion file:
[`keyword-map.json`](./keyword-map.json).

## Method and its limits — read this first

- Every claim below is cited to a URL that was actually searched or fetched during this research.
- **No search-volume figures were obtained.** Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs and Semrush were not
  available in this environment, and no credible third-party source published UK monthly volumes for
  these terms ([search attempt](https://uk.jobted.com/metal-finishing-jobs) returned listing counts
  only, not search data). Where a volume would normally appear, this document says
  **"no reliable figure obtained"**. Nothing here is estimated or invented.
- In place of volume, the demand proxy used throughout is **live vacancy counts on UK job boards**.
  This is a defensible proxy for a recruitment site (job supply tracks candidate search demand in a
  niche this small) but it is not search volume, and it should be replaced with real Keyword Planner
  data before any budget is committed.
- The search tool available is US-hosted, so SERP ordering is indicative rather than an exact render
  of a UK Google result page. Result *sets* were consistently UK-domain-heavy (uk.indeed.com,
  totaljobs.com, reed.co.uk, cv-library.co.uk), so the competitive picture is sound; exact positions
  should be re-checked from a UK IP before reporting rankings.
- Sector size anchor: HSE states there are **approximately 2,000 companies in the UK surface
  engineering industry**
  ([hse.gov.uk](https://www.hse.gov.uk/surfaceengineering/surfaceengineering.htm)). This is a small
  market. Treat all traffic expectations accordingly.

### C12 — NADCAP / AS9100 quality hiring

- **Intent:** commercial, employer-side, high-value.
- **Volume:** no reliable figure obtained (almost certainly very low; this is a long-tail play).
- **SERP:** **no recruitment page ranks.** The results are entirely (a) individual job adverts —
  [ASC Connections quality engineer, Wrexham](https://www.totaljobs.com/job/quality-engineer/asc-connections-ltd-job107902252),
  [Jobsite aerospace quality manager](https://www.jobsite.co.uk/jobs/quality-manager-aerospace)
  (Elevation Recruitment, Leicester, AS9100 + AS13100 + NADCAP) — and (b) **compliance-consultancy
  and accreditation explainers**:
  [Oxebridge](https://www.oxebridge.com/emma/nadcap-aerospace-special-processes/),
  [MPC](https://mpofcinci.com/blog/complete-nadcap-guide/),
  [Valence Surface Technologies](https://www.valencesurfacetech.com/the-news/nadcap-vs-as9100/),
  [Q-Hub](https://www.q-hub.app/regulations/nadcap).
- **Why it matters:** NADCAP accredits aerospace **special processes** — precisely plating, anodising
  and heat treatment — and requires AS9100 certification of the quality system before a NADCAP audit
  can occur ([Oxebridge](https://www.oxebridge.com/emma/nadcap-aerospace-special-processes/)). Every
  NADCAP-accredited UK finisher (e.g.
  [Metal Finishings Ltd](https://www.metalfinishingsltd.co.uk/quality/), supplying Thales, Cobham,
  Safran and MBDA) has a permanent, non-substitutable quality-hiring problem. Nobody is writing to
  it from a recruitment position.

  UK engineers due to retire by 2026; just 15% of engineers are under 30; 76% of engineering
  employers reported recruitment difficulty in 2025; Make UK reports 36% of manufacturing vacancies
  are hard to fill vs a 24% all-industry average

  [Rubicon People](https://rubiconpeople.co.uk/technical-and-engineering-skills-shortage/)).
- **Assessment:** generic, saturated, and everyone says the same thing. Only worth entering with
  **sector-specific** data (surface engineering retirement, HSE's accident-rate finding), never as a
  generic "skills gap" post.

### 2.7 SERP features observed

- **Job packs / Google Jobs:** the discipline SERPs are saturated with job-board index pages
  (Indeed, Totaljobs, Reed, CV-Library, Adzuna, Jobsite, Glassdoor, LinkedIn, JOB TODAY, Jooble,
  NewsNow, Jobted) — the signature of a query Google treats as a jobs query. Assume a job pack sits
  above the organic results on every `[process] jobs` term. **This is the single biggest constraint
  on the discipline pages** and the reason `JobPosting` structured data on `/jobs` matters more than
  any copy change.
- **DWP Find a Job** ranks organically on several discipline SERPs
  ([anodising](https://findajob.dwp.gov.uk/details/17469211),
  [electroplating](https://findajob.dwp.gov.uk/details/16643040),
  [surface finishing](https://findajob.dwp.gov.uk/details/15338659)) — an unusual signal that these
  SERPs are shallow enough for a `.gov.uk` job detail page to place.
- **Salary pages** rank on the higher-volume discipline terms
  ([Talent.com](https://uk.talent.com/salary?job=electroplating),
  [Reed average salary](https://www.reed.co.uk/average-salary/average-process-engineer-salary-in-london)).
- **Sitelinks:** present for the large job boards and for Aspion; not achievable near-term.
- **People Also Ask:** not directly observable through the available tooling — **no reliable
  observation obtained.** Do not assert PAA coverage in any client-facing document.
- **Local pack:** not observed on any of these queries. These are national, not local, searches.
  Do not build location landing pages on this evidence.

## 3. Content gaps Surface Talent can credibly own

Each gap is tied to something actually observed above.

1. **No UK agency page names a single surface finishing process.** Aspion's 2,500–3,000-word metals
   page mentions none; Precision People's mentions none. A `/disciplines/*` page that talks about
   rack vs barrel, sulphuric vs chromic vs hard anodising, or carburising vs nitriding is
   differentiated on the first screen.
   *(Evidence: [Aspion](https://www.aspion.co.uk/cm/specialisms/metals-engineering-recruitment),
   [Precision People](https://www.precision-people.uk/engineering/sheet-metal-recruitment).)*

2. **Nobody writes about NADCAP-qualified quality hiring for special-process shops.** The NADCAP
   SERP contains job adverts and compliance consultancies — zero recruitment content. A page
   explaining how to hire a quality engineer who can carry a NADCAP audit through a hard-chrome or
   anodising line, and why AS9100 certification must precede the NADCAP audit, has no competitor.
   *(Evidence: [Oxebridge](https://www.oxebridge.com/emma/nadcap-aerospace-special-processes/),
   [ASC Connections advert](https://www.totaljobs.com/job/quality-engineer/asc-connections-ltd-job107902252),
   [Metal Finishings Ltd](https://www.metalfinishingsltd.co.uk/quality/).)*

3. **Neither trade body serves recruitment.** SEA has no jobs board or skills section; the IMF has
   four manually-listed vacancies and no agency presence. The sector has no recruitment hub.
   *(Evidence: [sea.org.uk](https://sea.org.uk/),
   [materials-finishing.org/jobs](https://materials-finishing.org/jobs/).)*

4. **"Surface finishing recruitment" currently returns plasterers.** Google has no strong industrial
   document for the phrase. A correctly-scoped page could become the reference result.
   *(Evidence: SERP returned [Phoenix Gray](https://www.phoenixgrayrec.com/plasterer-recruitment-uk/),
   [Quick Placement](https://quickplacement.co.uk/workforce-hub/skilled-tradesmen-staffing-agency/plasterers-recruitment-agency),
   [Skills England surface finisher standard](https://skillsengland.education.gov.uk/apprenticeships/st0963-v1-0).)*

5. **No thermal spray recruitment landing page exists in the UK index.** VHR ranks on a single job
   posting; there is no HVOF/plasma hiring page from anyone.
   *(Evidence: [VHR job](https://www.v-hr.com/jobs/thermal-spray-coating-manager-16678/),
   [Glassdoor 7 jobs](https://www.glassdoor.co.uk/Job/thermal-spray-jobs-SRCH_KO0,13.htm),
   [LinkedIn 7 jobs](https://uk.linkedin.com/jobs/thermal-spray-jobs).)*

6. **Nobody connects HSE risk to hiring.** HSE reports surface engineering accident rates are
   "significantly higher than manufacturing as a whole", and names dermatitis, isocyanate asthma,
   nickel and chromium exposure and vibration white finger as sector-specific ill-health risks. No
   agency links this to EHS/SHEQ recruitment — but every operator has a COSHH problem and an
   EHS-hiring problem.
   *(Evidence: [HSE](https://www.hse.gov.uk/surfaceengineering/surfaceengineering.htm).)*

7. **Salary transparency at the technical grades.** Salary pages rank
   ([Talent.com](https://uk.talent.com/salary?job=electroplating)), but they report a blended
   £27,750 average that lumps operatives with technicians — against observed real bands of
   £34–38k (electroplater, Hayes), £29–34k (heat treatment shift leader), £31,470 (powder coating
   supervisor) and £50–60k (thermal spray manager). A grade-by-grade, process-by-process salary
   view is genuinely absent and genuinely useful.
   *(Evidence: [Totaljobs electroplating](https://www.totaljobs.com/jobs/electroplating/in-uk),
   [Totaljobs heat treatment](https://www.totaljobs.com/jobs/heat-treatment),
   [Reed powder coating](https://www.reed.co.uk/jobs/powder-coating-jobs),
   [VHR](https://www.v-hr.com/jobs/thermal-spray-coating-manager-16678/).)*

8. **Operator-owned credibility.** Surface Talent is a sister company of EMC Surface Technologies,
   which has invested in surface engineering businesses for 20+ years and owns operators including
   Karas, United Anodisers and RDM ([EMC Capital](https://emccapital.uk/emc-surface-technologies/)).
   No competing agency has an equivalent, verifiable operator lineage. This is an E-E-A-T asset that
   should be stated on `/about` with the corporate relationship made explicit and linkable.

## 4. Authority reality check

**No rankings are promised anywhere in this document.** The following is a judgement about
difficulty, not a forecast.

### Realistically winnable near-term (0–6 months)

- **C3 surface finishing / surface coatings recruitment** — the SERP is mis-served by plasterer and
  office-support agencies. Low competition because low, ambiguous volume. Cheap to take; will not
  deliver meaningful traffic.
- **C8 thermal spray** — no landing page exists from anyone in the UK. Winnable almost immediately.
  Expect a handful of visits, but the roles are £50–60k+ manager grade
  ([VHR](https://www.v-hr.com/jobs/thermal-spray-coating-manager-16678/)), so one placement pays for
  the whole SEO programme. Value, not volume.
- **C12 NADCAP / AS9100 quality hiring** — long-tail, zero recruitment competition, directly
  commercial. The highest-conviction near-term play in this research.
- **Brand and navigational queries** — "Surface Talent", "Surface Talent recruitment". Winnable by
  default; ensure `/about` states the EMC relationship so the entity resolves correctly.

### Winnable medium-term (6–18 months), with real work

- **C2 metal finishing recruitment / agency** — no incumbent owns it, but three different page types
  fragment the SERP and Google has not settled on an intent. Requires a genuinely deep page plus
  links from sector sources (IMF, SEA, trade press) to consolidate.
- **C4 electroplating and C5 anodising** — the job packs are shallow (23 and 4 live roles). A
  process-literate page can place organically *below the job pack*. The constraint is the job pack
  position, not the organic competition.
- **C7 heat treatment** — winnable on the *recruitment* modifier and on metallurgical long-tail
  (carburising, nitriding, vacuum furnace). **Not** winnable on the bare "heat treatment jobs" head
  term against 212 Glassdoor and 151 LinkedIn listings, and the
  [Heat Recruitment Ltd](https://www.reed.co.uk/jobs/heat-recruitment-jobs) brand collision will
  drag on it indefinitely.

### Long-term or not worth pursuing

- **C6 powder coating jobs — do not chase the head term.** CV-Library alone runs 189 live roles from
  103 employers, and Agency Central lists **60+ specialist industrial agencies** already supplying
  powder coaters, including Adecco and Manpower
  ([Agency Central](https://www.agencycentral.co.uk/agencysearch/industrial/skills/powdercoating/powdercoating.htm)).
  This is volume staffing at £12.21–£14.00/hour. Compete only on supervisory and technical grades.
- **C10 surface engineering jobs — the size is illusory.** Indeed's 1,597 is loose matching across
  Capgemini, universities and OEM R&D
  ([Indeed](https://uk.indeed.com/Surface-Engineering-jobs)). Most of that traffic is not Surface
  Talent's market. Target it for positioning, not conversion.
- **C1 surface engineering recruitment head term** — contested by Hays and a directory. Hays'
  backlink profile is not catchable, and Agency Central is a directory Google trusts for this
  query shape. Long-term at best; consider a paid Agency Central listing as a faster route to the
  same click.
- **C11 galvanising** — too thin (4 London roles on LinkedIn) and the roles observed are operatives
  and HGV drivers. Cover it on `/disciplines`; do not build a page.
- **C13 skills shortage thought leadership** — saturated by generalist agencies all citing the same
  Make UK and EngineeringUK statistics. Only enter with sector-specific data.

### Structural constraints to be honest about

1. **Job packs sit above organic on every `[process] jobs` query.** No amount of copy changes this.
   The mitigation is `JobPosting` structured data on `/jobs` and getting into the pack itself.
2. **Total market is ~2,000 UK companies** ([HSE](https://www.hse.gov.uk/surfaceengineering/surfaceengineering.htm)).
   Even complete dominance of every cluster here is a small absolute traffic number. Judge this
   programme on enquiry quality and placements, never on sessions.
3. **Domain authority is the binding constraint, not content.** Every gap identified is a content
   gap that any of these competitors could close in a week if they cared. The durable moat is
   process literacy plus links from IMF, SEA, *Surface Matters*, *IMFormation* and the trade press —
   which is a business-development job, not an on-page job.

## 5. Implementation notes (observations only — no changes made)

- **State of the working tree changed during this research.** At the start of this task `src/app/`
  had no `sitemap.ts` and no `robots.ts`. Both now exist as untracked files, along with
  `src/app/(site)/disciplines/[slug]/`, `src/lib/site/disciplinePages.ts` and
  `src/lib/site/structuredData.tsx` — i.e. the discipline detail pages are already being built in
  parallel with this research. Nothing in this document or in `keyword-map.json` was written by that
  work, and this task changed no file outside `docs/seo/`.
- **One conflict to resolve.** `DISCIPLINE_PAGES` in `src/lib/site/disciplinePages.ts` ships six
  slugs — `electroplating`, `anodising`, `powder-coating`, `heat-treatment`, `thermal-spray` and
  **`pre-treatment`** — and `src/app/sitemap.ts` submits all six at priority 0.7. This research
  recommends **skipping `pre-treatment`** (see the map entry for the evidence: no jobseeker uses the
  phrase, no job board maintains a listing page for it, and the adjacent term that does have demand
  is "shot blasting", which is operative-grade work outside Surface Talent's positioning). Either
  drop the slug, or keep the page and exclude it from `sitemap.ts` with `robots: { index: false }`.
  Submitting an indexable page for a query nobody searches spends crawl budget and adds a thin page
  to the site's quality profile for no return. This is a decision for the owner, not a change this
  task is scoped to make.
- `/jobs` is `force-dynamic` and currently renders the approved empty state until the Airtable
  source is configured (`src/app/(site)/jobs/page.tsx`). `JobPosting` structured data should be
  added at the same time as the live feed, not before — marking up an empty page is worse than
  nothing.
- Existing `metadata.title` values use the pattern `Page — Surface Talent`, which the map below
  preserves.
- The 14 disciplines and their exact copy live in
  `src/components/pages/disciplines/disciplinesData.ts`. The proposed discipline detail pages should
  expand that copy, not restate it, or `/disciplines` and its children will compete.
